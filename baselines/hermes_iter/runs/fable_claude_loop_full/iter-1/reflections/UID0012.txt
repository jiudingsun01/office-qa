I patched the existing `tb-budget-monthly-expenditures` skill rather than creating a new one, since by-agency expenditures live in the same front-of-issue budget section.

What I added (the generalizable lessons from this question):

- The Bulletin has a separate **"Expenditures by Agencies"** table — one line per cabinet department/major agency, fiscal-year rows plus monthly rows, in millions of dollars — distinct from the functional-category breakdown.
- For "highest/lowest spending Department in FY X" questions: take the argmax across **Departments only**, excluding independent agencies, interest on the public debt, and the Total column.
- A key disambiguation: "Department" means a cabinet department (Department of Defense), not the functional category "National defense and related activities" — the two figures differ slightly. Defense dominates in the 1950s, but the skill says to verify against the printed row rather than assume.
- Report nominal millions as printed, no unit conversion.

I also updated the skill's description so future runs trigger it on "highest spending Federal Department" phrasing.
