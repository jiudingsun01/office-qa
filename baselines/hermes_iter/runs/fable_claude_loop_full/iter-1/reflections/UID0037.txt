Skill recorded: `.claude/skills/tb-poe-data-label-charts/SKILL.md`.

**What went wrong:** I fetched the September 2007 bulletin from FRASER and rendered PoE page 5. The "Payroll Employment" chart is a quarterly bar chart whose subtitle already defines the metric — "(Average monthly change in thousands from end of quarter to end of quarter)" — with a printed data label above each bar. The "end of Q1 to end of Q2" value is simply each year's bar **II**: 219 (2004), 264 (2005), 124 (2006), giving mean 607/3 = 202.333. My 166.667 (= 500/3) came from a label-to-bar misalignment — reading 2004's Q3 bar (112) in place of its Q2 bar (219).

**Why it generalizes:** this Payroll Employment chart (and sibling data-labeled bar charts) recurs in every Profile-of-the-Economy section of modern bulletins, and the failure mode — pairing staggered data labels with the wrong quarter — will recur for any "read the bar for quarter X of year Y" question. The new skill captures: bar-N = change from end of quarter N−1 to end of quarter N (so no level arithmetic is needed), read printed labels rather than estimating or recomputing from external BLS data (vintage differs), and a transcribe-all-labels-then-count-then-chunk-by-year procedure to prevent one-slot shifts, with the Sept-2007 chart as a worked example.

Sources: [FRASER — Treasury Bulletin, September 2007 PDF](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/tbulletin/2007_09_treasurybulletin.pdf)
