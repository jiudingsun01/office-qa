The generalizable points it captures for future Treasury-Bulletin ESF questions:

- **Table location**: the ESF balance sheet is a recurring quarterly Treasury Bulletin section, published with a one-to-two-quarter lag, so "as of March 31" data lives in a later issue.
- **Units**: figures are printed in thousands of dollars; converting to billions rounded to the thousandth means dividing by 1,000,000.
- **Line-item precision**: "total nominal capital" (Capital section total) vs. "total capital and liabilities" (the grand total, equal to total assets) are distinct lines that are easy to conflate.
- **Question pattern**: the "value + absolute difference" two-part format, including subtracting before rounding and the `[x, y]` bracketed output convention.
