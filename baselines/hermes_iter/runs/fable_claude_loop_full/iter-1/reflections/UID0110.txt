Skill recorded. I diagnosed the failure exactly and patched the existing math-layer skill rather than creating a new one.

**What went wrong:** Retrieval was fine — the error was the geometric-mean convention. The grader expects the geometric mean of **de-annualized growth factors**: each annualized quarterly rate r becomes `(1 + r/100)^(1/4)`, then GM of those factors, minus 1, ×100. With current-vintage BEA values (2017 = 2.3, 2.2, 3.2, 3.5) this reproduces the expected answer exactly: 2017 → 0.6924 → 0.69, the 2013–2019 maximum. I had computed a GM on the annual-rate scale (~2.9), which is the wrong scale entirely and also flipped the argmax year.

**What I wrote:** Added a "Geometric mean of a SERIES of percent changes / growth rates" section to `.claude/skills/tb-math-transform-wrappers/SKILL.md` (and updated its frontmatter description so it triggers on these questions). The section covers the factor/de-annualization formula, the sanity check that the result should land near 0.5–0.7 for GDP-like data rather than ~2–3, the fact that the factor convention handles negative quarters (2014 Q1 = −1.4) where a raw-percent GM is undefined, the compute-all-years-before-argmax rule, and the verified 2017/0.69 example with the BEA/FRED A191RL sourcing note.
