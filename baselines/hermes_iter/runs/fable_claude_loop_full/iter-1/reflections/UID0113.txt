The generalizable lesson from this failure: my retrieval and per-year rates were evidently right (the absolute gap was 3.85 pp), but the grader wanted the **relative** difference — 3.85 divided by the base-year rate (~21.76%) gives the accepted 17.69. The phrase "relative difference in percentage points" means Δrate/base × 100, not plain subtraction, despite the "percentage points" wording.
