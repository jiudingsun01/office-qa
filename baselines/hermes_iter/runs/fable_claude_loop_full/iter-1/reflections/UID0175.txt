- **Recorded guidance**: the early Bulletins' "high-grade corporate bonds" column is the Bulletin's own published series and must be transcribed from the actual table, never substituted from FRED/Moody's Aaa memory; with ~6 two-decimal values the variance is ~1e-3, so a single ±0.01 cell misread corrupts the 4th–5th decimal — cross-check cells against the next issue's reprint; and compute both population and sample variance, suspecting a misread cell (not the divisor) when neither matches.
