2. **Multi-year ratio/mean questions** — "mean of the ratios for each year" means averaging per-year ratios, not sum(A)/sum(B); with WWII's exploding denominator the two methods give 0.68 vs 0.36, so the distinction alone can produce exactly this class of wrong answer. The skill now tells future runs to write out each year's numerator, denominator, and ratio before averaging.
