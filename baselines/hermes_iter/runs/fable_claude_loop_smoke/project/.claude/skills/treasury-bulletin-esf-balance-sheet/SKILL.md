# Exchange Stabilization Fund (ESF) balance sheet in the Treasury Bulletin

## Where to find it
- The ESF tables appear in the **International Financial Statistics** section of the Treasury Bulletin, under a heading like "Exchange Stabilization Fund" with a **Balance Sheet** table (and a separate Income/Expense table).
- Columns are quarter-end snapshots. "As of the last day of March YYYY" = the **March 31, YYYY** column (fiscal-quarter end). Match the column date exactly; adjacent columns show other quarter-ends.

## Units — the main pitfall
- ESF balance sheet figures are reported **in thousands of dollars**. To answer "in billions", divide the table value by **1,000,000** (e.g. 5,123,456 thousand → 5.123 billion).
- Round only at the end, to the precision the question asks (often "nearest thousandth").

## Mapping question wording to rows
- **"Nominal capital"** = the *Capital account* line in the Capital section (the fixed appropriated capital), NOT total capital. Total capital additionally includes the *Net income (accumulated)* / retained earnings line.
- **"Total capital and liabilities"** is the bottom-line total of the liabilities-and-capital side. By the balance-sheet identity it equals **Total assets** — use that as a cross-check when one of the two totals is hard to read.
- Liabilities section typically includes Special Drawing Rights (SDR) certificates / allocations and accounts payable; don't confuse SDR allocation liabilities with SDR holdings (an asset).

## Difference-type sub-questions
- "Absolute difference between X and Y": compute |X − Y| **after** converting both to the requested unit, then round. Don't round the inputs first.
